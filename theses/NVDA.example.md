# NVDA: accumulate on dips

Hyperscaler capex keeps growing through the next two years, and NVDA keeps most of the accelerator
market because of its software ecosystem. The forward P/E is justified if earnings keep growing
faster than the market. I would stop adding if data centre revenue growth falls below 20% year on
year, or if gross margin falls below 65%.
